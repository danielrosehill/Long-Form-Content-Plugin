description: Settle the brief and interview into the book spec

Write the spec.

Dispatch the `spec-writer` subagent, following the `book-definition` skill.

It produces `spec/spec.md`, updates `[book]` in `book.toml`, and sets
`status = "spec"`.

The two sections that carry the weight are **out of scope** — which is what stops
a twelve-chapter book becoming thirty during outlining — and **what it must not
become**. Every claim traces back to the brief or the interview; inferences are
marked as inferences.

Report the open questions the spec does not close rather than closing them by
assumption.
